Sample Ways to Reach Out (SAMPLE ONLY)
For a funeral home "Hi, this is Brent with Alice Marie's Catering. We make caring, home-style meals for families after a loss. Would it be okay if I brought by a small menu you could keep on hand for families who ask?"
For a church "Hi, I'm Brent with Alice Marie's Catering. I'd love to bring by a small tasting for your team and talk about how we could help with food for church events."

A note on funeral homes and grieving families: we never send an automatic sales message to a family after a loss. Care meals should only be shared through a trusted church or funeral-home relationship, a gentle referral card, or a personal introduction — never a sales follow-up.

Weekly Meal Prep

Three simple ideas to test — start small, see what people want.

1. Workday Lunch Club

Who may buy itPeople who work at one office, clinic, or church staff office
What they get5 ready-to-heat lunches delivered once a week to one work place
Sample price range (SAMPLE ONLY)About $70–$95 for 5 lunches
Pickup or delivery ideaOne delivery to the work place each week; needs at least 8–10 people ordering first
Why it could workOne stop can sell many lunches at once
One thing to watchNot enough people ordering can make delivery not worth the trip
Easiest way to test itAsk one office or church staff team to get 10 people to order for one Wednesday

2. Home-Style Weekly Meals

Who may buy itBusy families, church members, caregivers, working adults
What they get4 or 6 ready-to-heat home-style meals, with a family-size side you can add.
Sample price range (SAMPLE ONLY)$60–$76 for 4 meals; $84–$108 for 6 meals
Pickup or delivery ideaMonday pickup in Mableton; delivery on one nearby route
Why it could workEasy way for past catering customers to buy again
One thing to watchToo many special requests can slow down the kitchen
Easiest way to test itOffer just 4 meal choices to 20–30 people who already know Alice Marie's

3. Care & Comfort Meals

Who may buy itA family going through a funeral, sickness, new baby, or hard time
What they get6 single meals plus one shared family dish
Sample price range (SAMPLE ONLY)$95–$145 per bundle
Pickup or delivery ideaDelivery close by, or pickup
Why it could workMatches what people need after a hard time
One thing to watchThis must always stay caring — never feel like a sales pitch
Easiest way to test itAsk two churches and two funeral homes if they'll share a caring card with families who ask for help
REAL RESEARCH — Local examples we found: about $16–$18 per meal.
